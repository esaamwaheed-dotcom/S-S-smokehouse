Chapter I  —  The Name

A sire lights the fire.
His sons learn to keep it.

S&S stands for Sire & Sons — a family that measures time in hours of smoke, not minutes on a clock. What started around a backyard pit became a promise: cook it the way it was taught, and teach it the way it was cooked.

We're bringing that pit to Richardson. Central Texas technique, a table that welcomes every tradition, and a menu where brisket sits right next to bone-in lamb shoulder and saffron jollof, and all of it is halal. Same fire. Bigger family.

Chapter II  —  The Promise

Fully halal.
Fully Texas.

Texas barbecue has always been about who's welcome at the table. We built ours so everyone is. Every cut on the pit, every link of sausage, every side on the tray is halal. Nothing to ask about, nothing to check, nothing to skip.

  • Every meat is halalBrisket, beef ribs, lamb, chicken, and our house beef + lamb sausage.
  • No pork. Anywhere.Not on the menu, not on the pit, not in the beans.
  • Real Central Texas BBQSalt, pepper, post oak, and fourteen hours. Halal never meant settling.

Chapter III  —  The Pit

01

The Wood

Seasoned Texas post oak, split and stacked by hand. Clean blue smoke is the only seasoning that can't be bought.

02

The Rub

Salt. Coarse black pepper. And a family blend we'll take to the grave. Applied the night before, every single time.

03

The Fire

Two-fifty and holding. Through the night, someone is always awake, tending the fire the way they were taught.

04

The Rest

Wrapped in butcher paper and left alone. The bark sets. The juices settle. The hardest part is waiting.

05

The Slice

Against the grain, pencil-thick, a smoke ring you can see from across the room. Then it's yours.

The Signature

THEONE

There's only one sandwich we'd put our name on. Stacked with the best of the pit, built on a toasted bun, and finished with the house sauce. Order it once — you'll understand the name.
